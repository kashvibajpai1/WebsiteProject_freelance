Our Story

Four decades.
Two names. One loom.

It began in the 1980s with carpets — hand-knotted, one knot at a time, the way they have been made for centuries. What started as Weaves & Drapes grew knot by knot into a house trusted for textiles that carry a maker's hand in every inch.

Today, as Treasure Trove, we bring that same devotion to the handlooms of Varanasi — kadhua Banarasi silks, real zari tissue, shikargah brocades — sourced directly from weaver families we have known for a generation. No middlemen. No powerloom imitations. No shortcuts.

Every piece in our store has a provenance we can tell you over a cup of chai: which lane it was woven in, whose hands tied its knots, and why it took the time it took.

The Journey

A thread through time

Drag or scroll sideways to walk through our years.

1980s

The first knot

The house opens its doors as a carpet atelier — hand-knotted wool and silk, sourced from master weavers of Bhadohi and beyond.

1990s

Weaves & Drapes

Carpets are joined by textiles. The name Weaves & Drapes becomes a quiet word-of-mouth secret among collectors and homemakers.

2000s

The road to Banaras

Direct partnerships with handloom weaver families in Varanasi — kadhua, jangla, rangkaat — bringing the ghats' looms to our shelves.

2010s

A second generation

The next generation of the family joins, carrying the same rule: if a machine can make it, we don't sell it.

Today

Treasure Trove

A new name and a new home at CRC The Flagship, Sector 140, Noida — the same hands, the same looms, the same promise.

Why It Matters

The difference a hand makes

Knotted, not printed

Our carpets are tied knot by knot — up to 400 to the square inch. A single 8×10 can take a weaver most of a year. You can feel the difference with your eyes closed.

Real zari, real silk

Pure katan silk and tested zari from Varanasi's handloom karkhanas. Every Banarasi we sell is woven the kadhua way — each motif tied individually into the fabric.

Straight from the weaver

We buy directly from weaver families we've known for a generation. Fair prices at their end, honest prices at yours, and a story you can trace for every piece.
